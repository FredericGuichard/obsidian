comments from Reviewer:

1. First, the assumption of strict habitat dependence is a central weakness. While the manuscript explains this for both the detritivore in the brown-chain model and the consumer in the green-brown system, most results still focus on this restrictive, obligatory case. The biological implications of this assumption are not fully addressed. Since key results stem from detritus serving as both a resource and an obligatory habitat, the authors should either clearly show that the main conclusions remain valid for 0 < h < 1, or explicitly state that h = 1 is an extreme proof-of-concept and that real systems often involve partial dependence and multiple habitat sources.
